What Dynamic Bridge is testing

Not maximum autonomy. Reliable completion under explicit human control.

The proposed Execution Reliability Eval asks a production question that bounded task benchmarks often miss: did the intended real-world state become true, was it verified against the correct source of truth, and did that happen without context, permission, handoff, recovery, or duplication failure?
